ABOUT THE PROJECT

Technology for connected farming

FarmConnect is an academic prototype designed to make local farming knowledge easier to discover and share. It connects farmers with visitors and provides basic crop learning resources.

Planned future modules: verified agricultural expert consultations, map-based discovery, secure online payments, Marathi voice assistant, weather integration, and validated AI crop disease detection.
